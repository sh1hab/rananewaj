About

Trained in Dhaka, shaped by two decades of listening first.

Dr. Rana completed her MBBS at Popular Medical College and her FCPS in Internal Medicine under the Bangladesh College of Physicians and Surgeons, followed by a fellowship in diabetology. She now divides her time between hospital rounds and chamber consultations across the city.

Her approach centres on a simple idea: most chronic conditions are managed well only when the patient understands the plan. Every consultation ends with a written-down explanation, not just a prescription.
